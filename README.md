## 🛡️ Senior Software Technologist | CISSP

Hi there, I'm Jeremy Ho!👋 I bridge the gap between visionary software design and mission-critical execution. In the world of government services, "security by design" is a requirement, not an afterthought. I lead with a **DevSecOps-first mindset**, integrating **CISSP** principles into the DNA of the development lifecycle to ensure our citizen-facing systems are as impenetrable as they are reliable.

I am deeply passionate about improving the **developer experience**, believing that the best engineering cultures are built on tools that empower rather than abstract. My focus is on understanding the "why" to ensure we are building the _right_ thing and creating resilient systems that last. ~~As a large language model,~~ I also like to slap the keyboard and make cool things happen! 🙃🐠
